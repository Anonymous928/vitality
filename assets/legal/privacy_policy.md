# Afterglow privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Afterglow is a private journal for tracking post-orgasmic illness syndrome (POIS) symptoms. This policy explains what happens to your information. The short version: **everything you enter stays on your phone, and we never receive any of it.**

## The short version

- Afterglow has no internet permission. It cannot send anything you log anywhere.
- There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
- Your journal is stored encrypted on your phone.
- Only you decide if anything leaves your phone, for example by sharing a doctor report or exporting your data.

## What you enter and where it is stored

Afterglow stores the information you choose to enter:

- events you log (time, type, and optional details such as sleep, stress, alcohol and notes)
- check-ins (how you feel and symptom ratings)
- treatments you record (name, type, dose, prescriber and dates, as you entered them)
- your self-check answers
- your app settings

This is health information, including information about sexual activity, so we treat all of it as sensitive. It is stored only in an encrypted database on your phone. The encryption key is kept in your phone's secure key storage (Android Keystore).

## What we collect

**Nothing.** We, the developer of Afterglow, do not collect, receive, store, sell or share any of your information. Afterglow has no servers and requests no internet permission, so Android itself prevents it from going online. You can confirm this on the app's Google Play listing.

## When information leaves your phone

Only when you choose to send it, and only to where you choose:

- **Doctor report:** when you create a report, the PDF is made on your phone. You can then share it with an app you pick (for example email or a messaging app) or save it to a folder you choose.
- **Export:** you can export all of your data as a file. **This file is not encrypted.** Anyone who can open it can read it, so store it somewhere private.
- **Links:** community links, sources and support links open in your browser or another app. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Once you share or save a file, it is handled by the app or service you chose, not by Afterglow.

## Backups

Afterglow's data is excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. If you lose or reset your phone, or uninstall the app, your data cannot be recovered unless you exported it yourself.

## Permissions Afterglow uses

- **Notifications:** to remind you to check in after an event. The reminder text is neutral, can be changed in Settings, and is hidden on a locked screen.
- **Run at startup:** to restore your scheduled reminders after your phone restarts.
- **Biometrics / screen lock:** only if you turn on App lock. Afterglow never sees your fingerprint or PIN; Android checks it.
- **Vibration:** for reminder notifications.

Afterglow does not request internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage permissions.

## Crash and usage statistics

Afterglow contains no crash-reporting or analytics tools. If you have opted in to sharing usage and diagnostics with Google on your phone, Google Play may provide us with anonymous, aggregated crash and performance statistics. These never include anything you entered in the app.

## Deleting your data

- **Delete all data:** Settings, Delete all data. This permanently erases everything you have entered and cancels all reminders.
- **Uninstalling** Afterglow also deletes all of its data from your phone.

Because we never receive your data, we have no copy to delete, correct or hand over.

## Not medical advice

Afterglow is a self-tracking tool. It does not diagnose any condition or recommend treatment. Talk to a clinician about your health.

## Who Afterglow is for

Afterglow is intended for adults aged 18 and over.
